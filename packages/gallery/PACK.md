# Gallery package

For imagery-first pages where the interface recedes: moodboards, photo-heavy pages, visual collections.
Load with the installed DESIGN.md plus files from `refs/` chosen for the brief. Never load the whole refs folder.
Refs teach how to think, never what to build.

## Range map

- `refs/arena.md`: quiet archival grid; normalization lets heterogeneous media read as one collection.
- `refs/publicdomainreview.md`: ruled heritage index; disciplined taxonomy gives historical material navigable structure.
- `refs/colossal.md`: warm editorial mosaic; asymmetric scale shifts turn an art archive into paced storytelling.
- `refs/magnum.md`: museum-white photojournalism; varied crops and measured captions sustain a long photographic cadence.
- `refs/fontsinuse.md`: typographic specimen archive; compact metadata binds each image to its design context.
- `refs/walkerart.md`: graphic institutional gallery; strict grids let oversized identity coexist with commissioned art.
- `refs/cosmos.md`: immersive paper-light curation; subtraction gives expansive imagery and display type equal force.
- `refs/gagosian.md`: chromatic exhibition spectacle; artwork-responsive fields make each feature feel singular.

## Lessons (cross-site)

- Protect type through composition: reserve negative space, separate it onto a slab or field, or select imagery with clean contrast instead of defaulting to a blanket scrim.
- Let content govern color. Keep the shell restrained, then assign accents through artwork, taxonomy, or section meaning rather than a decorative global palette.
- First-glance impact comes from deliberate type relationships: distinct editorial and utility roles, decisive scale contrast, and controlled weight, leading, and tracking.
- Use scale, crop, and rhythm to create hierarchy; imagery-first pages gain more from editorial pacing than from decorated containers.
- Keep metadata terse but structurally consistent so captions, dates, makers, and categories orient the collection without competing with it.
- Limit chrome to a small, coherent shape vocabulary; square media edges and reserved control treatments keep interface from reading as content.

## Avoid (cross-site)

- Generic rounded-card masonry flattens archives and exhibitions into interchangeable inventory; preserve square content edges and purposeful editorial variation.
- Sparse, uniform, or weak imagery cannot carry image-led layouts; density, crop variety, and art direction are structural requirements.
- Do not copy mastheads, masks, rules, overlays, or brand colors as isolated motifs; signature devices only work with their original content logic and hierarchy.
