## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Put the product thesis in one compact live demo: the changing hero noun phrase communicates breadth without adding competing cards above the fold.
- Pair restrained charcoal neutrals with a single high-chroma pink family; reserve the strongest accent for commands, speed claims, and primary actions.
- Let technical proof follow the claim immediately: install commands, runtime examples, and benchmark bars turn positioning into inspectable evidence.
- Use a wide 1280px shell for navigation and comparison modules, but constrain explanatory prose to roughly 640–800px for readable technical scanning.
- Preserve product identity across structurally different surfaces: the docs switch to Inter/Paper Mono and a denser navigation system while retaining the dark ground and pink accent.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the animated typewriter plus blinking cursor without reduced-motion handling; perpetual motion can distract from the install action.
- Do not apply pink glow, drop shadows, and saturated syntax colors to every heading; their hierarchy works because most surrounding UI stays neutral.
