# biosecurity sector package

Use this pack for biosecurity, pandemic-preparedness, health-security, and biotechnology websites that need to establish scientific or institutional authority.
Load with the form package matching the page's job, plus 2-3 refs total chosen for the brief.
Refs teach how to think, never what to build.

## The biosecurity mode (avoid)

- Avoid opening on a blue-filtered PPE worker, futuristic globe, outbreak photograph, or rotating seasonal-health carousel paired with a broad preparedness mission and a generic “Learn More” or “Find out more” CTA.
- Avoid treating institutional blue as the entire credibility system, whether CDC blue `#0057B7` and teal `#0081A1`, Johns Hopkins navy `#002d72` and soft blue `#68ace5`, or CEPI navy `#00244F` alternating with white bands.
- Avoid the portal-like stack of three-column report cards, focus-area tiles, topic pills, A-to-Z directories, dated alerts, journal covers, and “Latest News” carousels.
- Avoid the stock visual vocabulary of PPE fieldwork, vaccine vials, virus particles, DNA strands, microscopy, laboratories, world maps, missiles, and circuitry, especially when unified only by blue filters or threat-category color coding.
- Avoid using repeated outline buttons, arrow links, and generic labels such as “Read more,” “See more,” “All News,” and “A-Z Index” in place of a clear action hierarchy.
- Avoid staging trust as a dense pre-footer ceremony of government strips, university marks, media names, coalition signs, policy links, language links, addresses, and mega-sitemaps.
- Avoid substituting unsupported scale theater for usable evidence through isolated counters such as `$12.5 trillion`, `Over 75`, and `470+`, a longevity claim, or a prominent donor testimonial.

## Tells

- A government or university masthead immediately followed by a blue global-threat photograph and a sweeping mission statement is the fastest sector-default tell.
- Navy-and-white bands filled with uniform report cards make threats, research, news, and programmes look like one institutional content feed.
- Blue-outlined topic pills, an A-to-Z index, dated alerts, and journal covers make the homepage read as a public-health portal.
- A coalition photograph, media-name list, or impact-number strip directly above a policy-heavy mega-footer signals that authority has been staged rather than demonstrated in the interface.

## Register

- Load-bearing trust comes from named reports, peer-reviewed papers or journals, attributable authors and experts, dated evidence, and inspectable technical detail; university marks, `.gov` explanations, outlet names, and coalition affiliation support that proof but do not replace it.
- The audience expects sober, precise, science-literate language, but the excellence references show that credibility does not require bureaucratic mission copy, crisis-blue fields, or a portal homepage.
- Make evidence operable and close to the claim, as Latch does with paper metadata and abstracts and Benchling does with bounded product interfaces, rather than burying it in a resource grid.
- Use hierarchy, reading measure, and a stable grid to carry authority: Asimov narrows articles to `60rem`, Ginkgo caps large copy at `64ch`, and the stronger systems keep expressive media inside disciplined content frames.
- One specific narrative device can create distinction - a molecular visual, indexed notebook grammar, organic squiggle, sticky preparedness sequence, or synchronized application carousel - while the rest of the system stays quiet and consistent.
- Scale and affiliation are credible when contextualized by named programmes, candidates, partners, publications, or methods; badges, decorative maps, and testimonial celebrity are secondary signals.
- **Operational surface:** keep named reports, papers with authors and dates, methods or product interfaces, and programme pages on the owned site. Latch puts paper metadata next to the claim; Benchling keeps product proof inside a bounded working surface.
- **Operational consistency:** citations, trial or programme names, and dates must resolve to a readable document. University marks and `.gov` chrome cannot replace the paper.
- **Photography:** keep molecular, organism, or lab imagery inside a reading frame with a caption job. Crisis-blue portals and decorative maps are the mode.
