## Token block (~10 lines)
- Palette - midnight #000650: primary text, headings, dark sections, and dark CTAs.
- Palette - bio blue #000DB5: brand/action fill; royal blue #2B39F1: links, eyebrows, and selected accents.
- Palette - powder blue #A9CAFB and ice blue #F0F6FE: secondary panels, tabs, borders, and text on dark brand fields.
- Palette - vanilla #F8F9EC and white #FFFFFF: quiet and default surfaces; rules gray #E6E6E6 and border gray #D6D7D1.
- Palette - electric lime #DAF972: high-contrast legacy CTA fill paired with #000DB5 text.

- Spacing rhythm - 4/8/10/12/16/20/24/32/40/56/64/80px recur; major homepage section offsets use 100px and 140px.
- Layout intent - centered 4-column mobile / 12-column desktop grid with 16px gutters, 1440px outer max-width, responsive side insets of 20/40/56px, and frequent 1104px content modules.
- Signature element - an off-canvas, shadowed animated product-UI window beside a fluid two-line hero, followed by a 1104px sticky headline/scrolling scientific-modality list.

## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Make technical authority feel approachable by pairing near-black scientific blue text with pale blue working surfaces, reserving brighter royal blue and lime for actions rather than flooding the page.
- Keep the core system rigid (12 columns, 16px gutters, repeated 1104px modules) while letting product media break the frame; the homepage hero media reaches 75% width and shifts 20% beyond the right edge.
- Use one sans family across editorial and product storytelling, then distinguish machine-like utility labels with a restrained mono face instead of introducing many decorative fonts.
- Translate complex platform breadth into interaction patterns: pill tabs for product families, a sticky explanatory headline for modality lists, and large animated software views for proof.
- Preserve readability across page types: both the product and customer-story interiors reuse 55/58px Graphik headlines and 18/22px supporting copy even though their hero structures differ.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the many blue variants without strict semantic roles; #000650, #000DB5, and #2B39F1 are close enough that arbitrary substitution would flatten hierarchy and weaken action cues.
- Do not reproduce the oversized off-canvas media or 100–140px section gaps without the mobile collapses present in the source; the site switches to stacked layouts, smaller type, and sharply reduced offsets below 768/1024px.
