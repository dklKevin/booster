## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Make advanced biology feel engineered rather than clinical: pair precise grotesk typography and restrained technical labels with one expressive molecular visual.
- Preserve a stable reading frame across marketing and science content: the site keeps an `80rem` shell, then narrows long-form material to `60rem` and increases body leading to `1.8`.
- Use saturated biotech color selectively: indigo carries links and controls while pale neutral, orange, and plum tints distinguish product content without replacing the white content ground.
- Let hierarchy do the credibility work: large `4.813rem` claims give way to `0.75rem` category labels, `1rem` body copy, and structured four-column product cards.
- Treat motion as a single narrative layer: the cellular hero/video and pinned product graphic provide the spectacle while the surrounding navigation, cards, and article templates remain typographically consistent.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the orb, purple-orange gradient, and blueprint motif without a similarly specific scientific story; together they are the site’s identifying visual device.
- Do not carry the desktop `5.5rem` gutters or four-column grid unchanged onto small screens; the fetched CSS reduces side padding to `1.25rem` at narrower breakpoints.
