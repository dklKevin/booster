- Palette: CDC blue is defined as #0057B7, link blue as #005EA2, cyan/teal as #0081A1, dark blue as #0B4778, near-black text as #1C1D1F, and pale cyan as #F4FBFC; the principal brand gradient runs #0057B7 to #0081A1, while the hero indicator strip runs #4C2B64 through #005EA2 to #0081A1.
- Type: Nunito is the 18px body sans-serif and is also used for buttons and links; Poppins is used for navigation, hero titles, overlays, and light-weight 35px section headings, with headings colored dark blue and underlined by a thin gray rule.

- Imagery: the hero source names and alt text verify topical editorial photos (an Ebola-prevention classroom chalkboard, hikers, and a person holding their stomach); lower modules mix editorial photography with branded journal cover graphics and an outbreak SVG, often using blue text overlays or blue gradient treatments.
- CTA pattern: action language is generic and repeated—“Learn More,” “All News,” “A-Z Index,” and “All Outbreaks”—rendered as blue/teal outline buttons or text links with right-angle icons; featured topics are presented as many small outlined buttons rather than one dominant conversion action.
- Trust signals: authority is staged through the U.S.-flag “official website” banner, explicit .gov and HTTPS explanations, the CDC wordmark, a self-description as the nation’s science-based and data-driven public-health organization, peer-reviewed journal cards, dated news, and HHS.gov/USA.gov links; no homepage accreditation badges or headline statistics were present in the fetched source.
- Footer weight: the footer is unusually deep—an About CDC mission block and nine-link organization list sit on a minimum-400px blue-to-teal gradient, followed by dark-blue expandable groups for contact, agency information, policies, 16 language-assistance links, and archives, then a gray logo/social band and a final white HHS.gov/USA.gov strip.
