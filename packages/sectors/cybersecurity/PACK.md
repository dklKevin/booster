# cybersecurity sector package

Use this pack for cybersecurity, identity, network defense, cloud security, and software-supply-chain sites that must establish technical and enterprise trust.
Load it with the form package matching the page's job, plus 2-3 refs total chosen for the brief.
Refs teach how to think, never what to build.

## The cybersecurity mode (avoid)

- Avoid the dark moving “AI security” hero with left-aligned white claims, abstract network or shield imagery, and paired “Learn More” and “Get a Demo” actions; observed canvases include `#290E23`, `#000`, `#141414`, and `#001744`.
- Avoid treating red, orange, magenta, or neon aqua as automatic threat-and-action signals, especially the recurring `#EC0000`, `#da291c`, `#FA582D`, `#e40c5b`, and `#6BFFB3` accents.
- Avoid the long enterprise procession of centered section intro, card deck, platform diagram, statistic band, carousel, logo wall, report cards, final demo blade, and mega-footer.
- Avoid literalized “unified platform” graphics built from concentric arcs, glowing chevrons, global threat maps, labeled capability icons, or blue architecture diagrams unless the diagram explains an actual system.
- Avoid polished executive or customer photography overlaid with glowing geometric shields, identity motifs, network globes, or dark gradients as generic evidence of protection.
- Avoid staging trust as a detached pile of Gartner and Forrester Leader claims, K/M/B/T counters, Fortune-scale customer counts, autoplay logo gardens, and rotating executive quotes.
- Avoid repeating generic arrow CTAs, pill buttons, report downloads, free trials, and demo requests until they culminate in a 45-57-link enterprise footer.

## Tells

- A dark AI-themed carousel makes an expansive platform promise beside glowing threat-tech art.
- A dense architecture diagram is followed by giant daily-threat metrics and a corporate logo wall.
- Gartner or another analyst quadrant occupies the hero or a dedicated recognition-card band.
- Alternating pale cards and dark proof carousels end in another demo CTA and a multi-column mega-footer.

## Register

- Credibility is load-bearing when the product or threat model is visible and inspectable, as in 1Password's dashboard, Socket's dependency graph, Tailscale's role-based network illustration, and Material Security's meaningful attack paths.
- Quantified proof earns space when it is tied to a named operational outcome, recognizable product artifact, customer role, or system behavior; isolated scale counters and logo acreage are decoration.
- The audience expects precise, composed, technically literate language, and Chainguard, Cloudflare, and Tailscale show that authority can come from grids, rules, mono labels, constrained copy, and calm spacing rather than ominous imagery.
- Analyst recognition can support a decision, but it should not substitute for product evidence; the mode sites repeatedly foreground Gartner and Forrester, while the excellence sites resolve claims into dashboards, package metadata, diagrams, and interactions.
- Trust does not require a black canvas or alarm color: warm `#E4E3E0`, `#f9f7f6`, white `#FFFFFF`, hairline borders, and restrained violet or blue actions can make security feel engineered, legible, and approachable.

## Range map

1password.com - Calm product-led assurance through a dark dashboard stage, disciplined blue transition, and generous soft geometry.
chainguard.dev - Optimistic engineering precision through a light blueprint grid, mono accents, and controlled vivid states.
cloudflare.com - Infrastructure expressed as the layout system through orange fields, dotted networks, and aligned dashed rails.
goteleport.com - Deep infrastructure drama through motion, related navies, pale-purple controls, and editorial hierarchy.
material.security - Warm editorial asymmetry that turns attack paths, graph cells, and state colors into the composition.
socket.dev - Developer-security evidence made tangible through an interactive dependency graph, package artifacts, and isolated alert gradients.
tailscale.com - Approachable Zero Trust storytelling through warm neutrals, low-shadow product panels, role tabs, and network illustrations.
wiz.io - Friendly enterprise conversion through a compact blue-led system, stable content spine, and tactile pink-shadowed actions.
