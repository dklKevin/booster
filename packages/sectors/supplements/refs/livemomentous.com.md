## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Reserve high-chroma color for conversion and evidence cues: the mostly black/white system makes `#FF5E03` actions and the campaign-specific `#7DD3FC` hero CTA unmistakable.
- Pair restrained 16/24px explanatory copy with compact, near-solid display headlines (30/36px to 60/60px); this gives technical claims authority without making dense supplement information hard to scan.
- Let product proof define the hero: the live homepage names a quality standard, shows the product at full bleed, and places the claim, supporting benchmark copy, and CTA in one view.
- Carry one responsive grid logic across marketing and commerce: single-column mobile sections become equal two-column compositions at 768px, while gutters expand before content reaches the 1920px outer cap.
- Use warm grays (`#FBFBFA`, `#F6F6F5`, `#E2E2E0`) for product cards, dividers, and controls so clinical information feels softer than a pure monochrome interface.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the 60/60px hero type or full-bleed dark imagery without similarly short claims and high-contrast assets; longer supplement copy will crowd the overlay.
- Do not deploy every extracted orange, blue, cyan, green, and yellow scale at once; the live pages achieve hierarchy by using the broad token inventory selectively.
