## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Let branded packaging and campaign art carry the category color while the commerce shell stays almost entirely black, white, and pale gray; this keeps frequent flavor/collaboration launches from fighting the interface.
- Use extremely tight display leading and negative tracking for short, high-impact claims, but keep product facts, controls, and prices in the sturdier `15px` body face.
- Separate dense shopping utilities from product storytelling: the `213px` collection rail and sticky `36%` PDP panel preserve filters and purchase choices while imagery remains dominant.
- Repeat the unusually tight `6px` gutter across category tiles, carousels, and PDP media so mixed campaigns still read as one deliberate visual system.
- Scale whitespace in clear jumps (`20px` to `40px` to `80px`) to alternate compact commerce controls with expansive editorial sections.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the oversized, tightly led headlines for long supplement names or compliance copy; the source limits that treatment to short campaign statements and uses smaller H3/body styles for product detail.
- Do not reuse the accent colors as broad UI fills: the source relies on `#000000`, `#ffffff`, and `#f2f2f2` for structure and reserves brighter colors for accents and campaign content.
