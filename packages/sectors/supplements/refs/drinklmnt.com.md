## Token block (~10 lines)
- Core palette: #000000 for navigation/dark bands, #171717 for near-black sections, #FFFFFF for primary light surfaces, and #FAFAFA for off-white sections.
- Supporting neutrals: #EDEDF0 for pale panels, #D4D4D4 for copy on dark, #A3A3A3 for muted copy, #525252 for secondary light-surface copy, and #4D4D51 for product UI text.
- Accent palette: #FFF200 lemonade yellow for the current campaign/get-yours CTA, #AFC13D greencta-500 for action emphasis, and #9EAA54 citrus-700 for citrus product surfaces.
- Body stack: Inter, sans-serif at 12/16px, 14/20px, 16/24px, 18/28px, and 20/28px; regular and bold font files are self-hosted.
- Display pairing: GothamSsm-Book and GothamSsm-Bold with ui-sans-serif/system fallbacks; headings commonly use 24/32px and a custom 32px/1.1 scale with .03em tracking.
- Large display scale: 36/40px, 48/48px, and 72/72px; the shared .h1–.h6 scale is 72, 60, 48, 36, 30, and 24px with -.02em tracking.
- Spacing rhythm: 4px-derived utilities, with recurring 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, and 80px gaps/padding; section bands commonly use 48px vertically, increasing to 80–96px at larger breakpoints.
- Content widths: 64rem, 80rem/1280px, and 1200px reading/product shells; full-bleed media is capped at 2000px.
- Layout intent: full-width campaign and dark editorial bands contain centered 16–32px-gutter shells, shifting from one column to 3-column editorial grids or 5-column product grids.
- Signature element: an oversized exploded stick-pack composition (the product source names it “Exploding Salt Pack”) that turns formulation details into a dramatic product visual.

## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Pair a restrained black/white editorial frame with one flavor- or campaign-coded accent; this makes frequent launches feel new without replacing the underlying system.
- Put the formulation claim in the information hierarchy, not only in fine print: repeated “More Salt, Not Less,” “What’s inside?”, benefit icons, and science-card sections connect commerce to evidence.
- Let product media break out to 2000px while keeping copy and controls within 1200–1280px shells; the contrast creates spectacle without sacrificing scanability.
- Use Gotham SSm selectively for compact, forceful headings and CTAs while Inter carries dense product, review, FAQ, and account UI.
- Alternate full-bleed campaign imagery, near-black proof/story bands, and pale commerce sections to create a clear long-page cadence.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not reuse #FFF200 everywhere: the fetched homepage applies it to the Lemonade campaign and primary CTA, while product variants use their own accents.
- Do not copy the oversized product imagery without the narrow content shells and 48–96px section spacing; otherwise the page will read as an undifferentiated image stack.
