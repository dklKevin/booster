## The supplements mode (avoid)

- Avoid the full-width promotional carousel with separate desktop/mobile campaign art, oversized discount copy, and an all-caps “SHOP NOW” CTA baked into the image.
- Avoid default performance palettes such as `#3b60ff` on `#111111`, `#FFE500` on `#000000`, `#e60d2e` on white, or `#d13239` fading into black.
- Avoid stacking category scrollers and repeated BEST SELLERS, PREWORKOUT, or LATEST RELEASES carousels filled with isolated tubs, cans, ratings, badges, swatches, and quick-add controls.
- Avoid alternating catalog pack renders with blue-lit gyms, muscular training photography, fruit-and-liquid splashes, powder pours, and red-to-black product composites as the only visual cadence.
- Avoid treating bold uppercase condensed type as a substitute for hierarchy, especially when every offer, section title, and CTA receives the same emphasis.
- Avoid staging trust chiefly through shipping thresholds, secure-payment icons, subscription savings, loyalty points, generic star counts, and borrowed-brand collaborations.
- Avoid the dense near-black footer that combines newsletter capture, link columns, payment marks, accessibility badges, app links, policies, and a bordered or full-width FDA disclaimer.

## Tells

A discount-first image hero pairs enlarged packaging with huge offer typography and a baked-in “SHOP NOW” button.
Horizontal product rows repeat isolated pack shots, stars, sale badges, flavor choices, and quick-add controls.
Black, red, yellow, or electric blue performance styling surrounds condensed uppercase claims and gym or splash imagery.
Shipping, secure checkout, review totals, and subscription savings appear as the primary proof before a disclaimer-heavy dark footer.

## Register

- Credibility depends on formulation and quality evidence in the main hierarchy: exact-dose or transparent-label information, third-party verification, testing standards, research, and named proof pillars are load-bearing.
- Shipping offers, secure checkout, loyalty rewards, review totals, payment marks, and accessibility badges reduce purchase friction but remain supporting commerce reassurance, not scientific proof.
- Use direct, calm explanatory copy for facts and controls; reserve forceful condensed or ownable display type for short claims, product names, and campaign ideas.
- Keep evidence inside the same grids, cards, accordions, and split panels as commerce so traceability feels integral rather than like a detached compliance report.
- Excellence can range from cinematic product science to warm editorial rigor, but it consistently uses a stable neutral shell, selective accents, disciplined whitespace, and distinct storytelling and transactional modes.
