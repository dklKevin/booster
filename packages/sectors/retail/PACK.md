# retail sector package

Use this pack for stores, catalogs, product-detail pages, and retail content that must support discovery, evaluation, and purchase.
Load with the form package matching the page's job, plus 2-3 refs total chosen for the brief.
Refs teach how to think, never what to build.

## The retail mode (avoid)

- Avoid opening on a rotating percentage-off carousel or rounded split hero with offer copy beside a campaign image, as seen in Michaels' 50/50 slides and PetSmart's `12px` coupon block.
- Avoid the endless merchandising feed of category tiles, logo rows, promotional banners, product carousels, service cards, and repeated two-to-six-column grids used across Chewy, Michaels, and PetSmart.
- Avoid defaulting to brand sans stacks and mixed legacy UI faces such as Gordita with broad fallbacks, CircularStd plus Inter, or Euclid alongside Arial and Helvetica.
- Avoid the familiar commerce palette split of saturated action color on white and pale gray: Chewy uses `#1c49c4`, `#f06c00`, `#ededed`, and `#ffffff`; Michaels uses `#CF1F2E`, `#F8F8F8`, and `#FFFFFF`; PetSmart uses `#206EF6`, `#F7F7F7`, and `#FFFFFF`.
- Avoid making rounded pills labeled "Shop Now," "Shop all," or "Learn more" the main source of hierarchy; Michaels uses a `100px` button radius and PetSmart repeats short transactional labels across campaigns.
- Avoid the standard image mixture of isolated packshots, square category crops, brand-logo tiles, staged lifestyle scenes, and offer banners without a deliberate role for each genre.
- Avoid staging trust as a pre-footer bundle of discounts, rewards, assortment counts, shipping thresholds, pickup speed, Autoship, price matching, service cards, badges, and email capture; Chewy, Michaels, and PetSmart all concentrate reassurance this way.
- Avoid the dense retail-directory ending by reflex; PetSmart closes with four columns and 21 navigation links, two badges, social icons, and six legal links, while Michaels demonstrates that a lighter footer is possible.

## Tells

- The first viewport is an expiring percentage-off offer paired with a generic "Shop now" control.
- Category tiles hand directly to promotion cards, logo strips, and a horizontal product carousel.
- Packshots, lifestyle photography, deal callouts, and brand marks all occupy the same rounded card system.
- Rewards, delivery promises, service counts, and signup incentives arrive as the confidence layer near the bottom.

## Register

- Credibility comes from helping shoppers retrieve and evaluate the right item: persistent search and taxonomy at Adafruit and McMaster-Carr, large technical media at Adafruit, and stable buying rails at Adafruit and Sweetwater are load-bearing.
- Expertise must enter the transaction itself through task guidance, specifications, safety and guarantee content, repair help, or a reachable named advisor; generic service claims and decorative headshots do not carry the same weight.
- Certifications are load-bearing where the transaction is regulated, as PetSmart's LegitScript and .pharmacy badges show; rewards multipliers, assortment counts, and email incentives are secondary unless they change the purchase decision.
- Use plain task labels such as "Search," "Cart," "Repair Help," "Ingredients," "Instructions," and "Add to Cart" so voice never obstructs wayfinding.
- The audience expects compact, direct, informed language with prices, availability, fulfillment, and support visible at the point of decision.
- Excellence proves retail can lead with maker culture, education, editorial inspiration, expert service, or problem-solving while preserving operational commerce and template-specific information architecture.
- **Operational surface:** keep search, taxonomy, product detail with price and availability, cart, fulfillment, and support or repair help on the owned site. Adafruit, McMaster-Carr, and Sweetwater make retrieval and evaluation load-bearing.
- **Operational consistency:** one current source for price, stock, shipping, and specs. A campaign price that disagrees with the product page is a broken store.
- **Photography:** give packshots, technical media, and lifestyle distinct jobs. King Arthur treats food photography as structure; isolated packshots in the same rounded card as deal banners is the mode.

## Range map

- adafruit.com - maker culture joined to dense technical commerce and direct purchasing.
- aquariumcoop.com - quiet catalog confidence with education treated as retail infrastructure.
- kingarthurbaking.com - editorial inspiration and transaction held inside one coherent brand system.
- leevalley.com - disciplined tool-catalog utility with restrained editorial collection cues.
- mcmaster.com - expert retrieval, visual taxonomy, and specification density at industrial scale.
- parktool.com - specialist retail organized equally around equipment and completing the repair.
- rei.com - campaign emotion bridged into structured outdoor merchandising.
- sweetwater.com - high-information product evaluation anchored by continuous human expertise.
