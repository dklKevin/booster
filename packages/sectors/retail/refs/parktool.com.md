## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Organize a specialist retailer around the customer’s two strongest intents - buying equipment and completing a task - then make those paths the first interaction instead of treating education as a secondary footer link.
- Keep brand drama at the entry point, then shift interior commerce pages to repeatable, dimensioned product tiles and a restrained `1400px` content frame so large inventories remain scannable.
- Use one technical type family in purposeful cuts: black uppercase for hierarchy, roman for readable instruction, and condensed faces for dense navigation and captions.
- Connect commerce to expertise with parallel structures: product-category tiles for shopping and a dedicated two-column Repair Help search/results layout for problem solving.
- Make brand blue functional as well as decorative: it marks links, hover states, navigation indicators, section flags, category labels, and header gradients.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the split-screen hero unless the catalog genuinely supports two equally important user intents; otherwise the forced `50/50` choice and `65/35` expansion add friction.
- Do not reproduce the very large uppercase display scale or `100px` desktop gutters without the site’s responsive reductions to `50px` and `20px`; dense retail content would become cramped or overflow on smaller screens.
