## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Let one brand system serve inspiration and transaction: the homepage interleaves editorial hero, campaign, recipe, video, category, and product modules while preserving the same type, eyebrow, button, and color grammar.
- Treat food photography as structural content: responsive homepage hero sources range from 480x550 to 2000x944, while product and campaign imagery keeps intentionally different aspect ratios instead of forcing one universal card shape.
- Give task pages their own information architecture: the recipe page pairs a compact stats/action band with ingredients and spacious numbered instructions, rather than reusing the homepage's promotional module stack.
- Use the 1360px frame as a shared alignment rail, then selectively break to full viewport width for high-energy color and imagery; this creates retail drama without losing browsing rhythm.
- Make small brand cues do repeated work: the red-flanked eyebrow and short red list rules connect navigation, metadata, articles, recipes, and merchandising without requiring every component to be red.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the large #da1a32 and #fbb040 fields without similarly strong, art-directed food photography; the source balances saturated color with dense photographic texture and white breathing room.
- Do not flatten the site's varied modules into a uniform card grid: its hierarchy depends on deliberate shifts among full-bleed hero, asymmetric campaign layouts, wide feature cards, product rows, and task-specific recipe columns.
