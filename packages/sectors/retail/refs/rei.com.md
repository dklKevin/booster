## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Pair a durable commerce sans with a restrained editorial serif: Graphik carries dense navigation, filters, controls, and product copy while Stuart gives campaign and section headings an outdoors-magazine voice.
- Keep the retail framework neutral and reserve saturated color for meaning: #1f513f marks brand/primary actions, #406eb5 links and active states, and #c7370f sale messaging.
- Let campaign imagery create emotion, then overlap it with a tightly structured 2/3/4-column shopping module; the 32-64px lift makes merchandising feel connected to the story rather than appended below it.
- Reuse the same 1232px content measure and 16/32px gutters across the homepage, global navigation, coupons, and category content so editorial modules and utilitarian shopping pages share one frame.
- Scale density by viewport instead of merely shrinking: the source changes grid count, gutters, card packing, heading family/size, and horizontal-scroll behavior at its responsive boundaries.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the oversized editorial type or image-first lead rail without REI's dense Graphik utility layer; otherwise basic shopping tasks will lose hierarchy and scan speed.
- Do not reuse the overlapping card anatomy as decoration on every section; its negative offset works because it explicitly bridges a lead campaign and the first merchandising block.
