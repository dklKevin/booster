## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Give a technical startup one proprietary display face and reserve it for a restrained 68px/44px headline tier; keep dense product explanations in a highly legible 17px/26px UI sans.
- Build the story from a stable 67rem shell and repeat one two-column geometry, then create variety through product animation and illustration instead of changing the grid each section.
- Use near-white #FBFAF9 panels and 12px radii as visual stages for screenshots; the low-contrast surface keeps colorful interface states legible without turning the page into a card mosaic.
- Let spacing signal narrative importance: 4.5-8.5rem section bands on desktop, while controls and copy stay on a compact 12/16/24/32px rhythm.
- Make social proof feel like product motion: Family's slow 120s marquee uses varied-width testimonial panels and edge fades rather than a static logo strip.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the many illustration accent colors without the dominant #343433/#FBFAF9 neutral system; the result would become noisy rather than playful.
- Do not hide a major proof section on mobile as this implementation does for testimonials at 768px; preserve a reduced static version if social proof is essential to conversion.
