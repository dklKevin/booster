## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Give an operational product a memorable editorial voice: the 80px serif headline carries the story while the sans face keeps controls, proof, and product detail precise.
- Reserve one saturated color for the conversion and narrative spine; #f25533 links buttons, emphasis, gradients, and the 2px editorial quote rule without turning every surface orange.
- Layer width constraints by job: 1,512px navigation shell, 1,192px section grid, and 700–840px reading measure create scale without sacrificing comprehension.
- Demonstrate complexity inside calm containers: warm neutral section fields, 8–16px radii, subtle #16161814 borders, and a two-part shadow (0 1px 2px plus 0 5px 14px at #0a14211a) make dense UI examples approachable.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the 80px/1.0 serif headline without its responsive fallback to 44px/1.1; the extracted homepage explicitly switches from H2 sizing to H1 at larger breakpoints.
- Do not spread the alarmalade gradient across routine content; its impact depends on white and warm-neutral sections dominating the page and orange marking only key moments.
