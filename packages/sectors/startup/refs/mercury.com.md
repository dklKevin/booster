## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Let a single cinematic interaction carry the brand: Mercury concentrates its strongest motion in the sticky hero, then returns to reusable grid, accordion, testimonial, and wayfinding modules.
- Make product breadth navigable rather than merely claim it: the hero continues into labeled video flows for banking, cards/expenses, payments/invoicing, and accounting.
- Build expressive variety from semantic themes: the same background/surface/text/border roles switch among dark neutral, neutral, beige, and purple sections and across About and Pricing.
- Pair a distinctive display face with restrained body/UI typography: Arcadia Display supplies personality while Arcadia keeps forms, navigation, and dense financial content legible.
- Keep conversion inside the visual thesis: the email field and “Open account” action sit directly in the hero, with a separate demo route, instead of waiting for a generic closing CTA.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the 250vh video treatment without responsive start frames, reduced-motion handling, masks, and mobile-specific sources; the source includes all four to control cost and accessibility.
- Do not reproduce the broad accent palette ad hoc; its colors work because every section maps them through consistent semantic surface, text, icon, border, hover, and active roles.
