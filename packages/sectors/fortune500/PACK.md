# fortune500 sector package

This pack applies to enterprise homepages and corporate routing pages for large, multi-business companies.
Load it with the form package matching the page's job, plus two or three references chosen for the brief; references teach how to think, never what to build.

## The fortune500 mode (avoid)

- Avoid full-bleed carousels with white mission copy, a dark wash, and generic CTAs over rotating operational or lifestyle photography, as seen at ExxonMobil, GE, Walmart, and P&G.
- Avoid making one corporate color the system, whether ExxonMobil red `#FE000C`, Walmart blue `#0053E2`, J&J red `#eb1700`, or P&G blue `#003da5`, against white and pale-gray bands.
- Avoid proprietary-sans hierarchy reduced to oversized light or heavy headlines, uppercase letter-spaced eyebrows, and semibold interface copy.
- Avoid the stock stack of hero, newsroom cards, business or brand carousel, sustainability feature, Careers and Investor Relations tiles, and final link grid.
- Avoid the interchangeable imagery of engineers, laboratories, offshore assets, turbines, clinical technology, employees, community portraits, products, and staged lifestyles.
- Avoid piling up stock tickers, quarterly results, ESG reports, scale claims, awards, philanthropy totals, and subsidiary marks before clarifying the visitor's task.
- Avoid repeated “Learn more,” “Read more,” and “Explore” pills, followed by a footer crammed with brands, policies, privacy controls, investor links, legal notices, and ticker disclaimers.

## Tells

- A cinematic rotating hero pairs aspirational copy with a dark gradient, white type, and one generic action.
- A stock strip or award claim occupies or immediately follows the hero.
- News, ESG, Careers, Investor Relations, and business divisions become equal card bands.
- A logo-heavy, compliance-heavy footer functions as a governance directory.

## Register

- Load-bearing trust comes from verifiable scale, current results with ticker disclaimers, named businesses, specific disease or product evidence, and direct access to reports and policies.
- Awards are decoration unless attributable; P&G's Fortune claim and J&J's TIME partnership work because each names its source and distinction.
- The expected tone is calm and exact, so concrete jobs and evidence should replace superlatives and broad purpose claims.
- Brand need not flood the canvas: Caterpillar confines `#ffcd11` to a 40px × 5px bar and states, while IBM, Coca-Cola, Deere, Nike, and Target rely on neutrals.
- Credibility rises when the primary job leads, as Delta's booking workflow and Deere's dealer, search, compare, offer, and breadcrumb paths prove.
- Responsive discipline signals control through Deere's breakpoint-specific crops, IBM's 4/8/16-column grid, and Coca-Cola's tokenized 56px-to-120px module rhythm.
- **Operational surface:** lead with the company's actual job: Delta's booking workflow, Deere's dealer, search, compare, and offer paths, or a current report and policy index. Purpose slogans cannot replace those tasks.
- **Operational consistency:** tickers, product names, and policy links must stay current and attributed. Awards belong only when the source and distinction are named, as with P&G and J&J.
- **Photography:** let product or campaign art carry one idea, then return to task UI. Full-bleed purpose films that never reach a booking, dealer, or shop path are a brochure.

## Range map

caterpillar.com - Represents industrial authority through a black frame, restrained yellow states, condensed type, and selective full-bleed photography.
coca-colacompany.com - Represents portfolio editorialism through a neutral shell, controlled accents, generous pacing, and an interactive 50/50 story system.
deere.com - Represents product-led enterprise design through responsive machinery art direction and commerce-grade wayfinding.
delta.com - Represents task-first service design through a navy booking workflow, consequential red actions, and cinematic content.
ibm.com - Represents dense enterprise systems through a disciplined 4/8/16-column grid and asymmetric leadspace.
nike.com - Represents campaign-led commerce through monochrome UI, tokenized display scale, full-bleed media, and consistent pills.
target.com - Represents approachable commerce through compact type roles, 4px spacing, restrained red, and circular brand geometry.
