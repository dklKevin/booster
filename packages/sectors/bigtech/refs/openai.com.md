## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Make the flagship product interaction the homepage's first visual object: the composer communicates capability faster than a slogan while the prompt pills expose distinct entry paths.
- Use a restrained black/white editorial system as the stable frame, then reserve the five extracted hue accents and rich imagery for product or story-specific moments.
- Build hierarchy with a compact 500-weight sans scale and tighter negative tracking, not many font weights; introduce LF Serif only when an editorial voice needs contrast.
- Let spacing expand dramatically between story groups (`80–120px`) while keeping controls on the `4px` unit and page edges at `24–32px`; this preserves density locally and calm globally.
- Keep article and homepage structures related but not identical: the shared `90rem` shell supports immersive media, while `65rem`/`65ch` constraints restore reading comfort on interior editorial pages.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not imitate the near-monochrome palette without the large media, deliberate type scale, and generous section gaps; it would read as unfinished rather than restrained.
- Do not transplant the ChatGPT-style composer as decoration: without a real or clearly useful interaction, its familiar textarea-and-send affordance creates a false promise.
