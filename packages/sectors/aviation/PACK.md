# aviation sector package

Use this pack for airline booking, aircraft and propulsion programs, advanced air mobility, and private-flight services.
Load with the form package matching the page's job, plus 2-3 refs total chosen for the brief.
Refs teach how to think, never what to build.

## The aviation mode (avoid)

- Avoid the full-screen autoplay aircraft reel with one shadowed white purpose statement, as in Airbus's 4rem headline over hangar footage.
- Avoid making a dense Roundtrip/From/To/Dates/Travelers/Cabin search panel the automatic hero merely because Lufthansa and United do.
- Avoid reflexive corporate navy fields such as #071A3D, #05164d, or #002244 alternating with white and pale blue-gray bands such as #F2F5F9.
- Avoid the standard stack of promotional carousels, mission panels, employee portraits, news cards, ancillary-service rows, and split CEO-quote blocks.
- Avoid the familiar imagery mix of cinematic aircraft operations, centered crop-and-fill story thumbnails, cabin products, destination lakes, packing travelers, and airport-lifestyle scenes.
- Avoid generic chevron actions such as “Discover more” and “Find out more,” or making every image-and-copy teaser a link.
- Avoid postponing trust to a heavy footer crowded with payment rails, passenger-rights links, social actions, portals, disclaimers, and alliance marks.

## Tells

- Full-bleed aircraft footage carries a world-scale sustainability or safety promise.
- Airline-blue utility UI puts the booking engine ahead of any distinctive point of view.
- Alternating navy and pale bands funnel every subject into another carousel, card grid, or chevron CTA.
- Workforce totals, investor KPIs, payment marks, and alliance logos arrive as a concentrated institutional proof layer.

## Register

- Transactional airline credibility depends first on lucid access to booking, check-in, flight status, fares, passenger rights, and payment methods; alliance logos are supporting assurance, not the story.
- Manufacturer credibility requires attributable operating evidence such as fleet activity, configurations, workforce and geography totals, or financial figures, with telemetry and fine rules making proof usable rather than ornamental.
- The expected voice is calm, precise, and operationally confident, with aspiration confined to short display statements while readable body and UI faces carry explanation and action.
- Image contrast and mobile behavior are trust issues: gradients, overlays, constrained copy measures, reduced hero heights, and linear fallbacks must preserve legibility and access.
- The excellence set proves aviation need not default to corporate blue: disciplined monochrome, warm cream and navy, controlled violet, or a single yellow accent can remain credible when each color has a fixed role.
- Cinematic scale earns attention only when paired with technical taxonomy, live activity, configuration data, bilingual clarity, or a direct planning task.
- **Operational surface:** put booking or flight planning, check-in, status, fares, fleet or configuration facts, and passenger rights on the owned site. VistaJet places Plan a flight under the promise; STARLUX keeps booking and loyalty in the same shell as editorial interiors.
- **Operational consistency:** one current source for routes, cabin names, fares, and schedules. Cinematic aircraft footage cannot substitute for a working planning task.
- **Local access:** name the airports, terminals, and lounges that actually apply, and keep bilingual wayfinding when the airline sells in more than one writing system, as Riyadh Air and STARLUX do.
- **Photography:** treat aircraft, cabin, and destination images as product evidence with a job, not interchangeable lifestyle filler under a dark overlay.
