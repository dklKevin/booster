## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Put the operational promise over cinematic aircraft footage, then place the flight-planning form immediately below it; the archive pairs “Flying private made simple” with “Plan a flight” before lifestyle storytelling.
- Separate aspiration from utility typographically: large Garamond/Albra display copy carries luxury, while compact Museo Sans handles dense fleet facts, forms, navigation, and calls to action.
- Keep brand recognition concentrated: `#b00016` appears on links, micro-labels, control states, and hover feedback while most surfaces remain `#fff`, `#f8f8f8`, or photographic.
- Reuse one predictable editorial module - alternating 50/50 image and copy cards - to explain fleet, dining, service, sustainability, and member benefits without changing the reading model.
- Let vertical space signal premium pacing: recurring `40px` and `70px` section gaps, with `140px` reserved for the largest breaks, keep information-rich pages from feeling like catalogs.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the `12px` legacy paragraph rule or wide uppercase tracking indiscriminately; the later body rule is `14px/1.8`, and `.2em`–`.35em` tracking is reserved for headings and short promises.
- Do not reproduce the video hero without the dark overlay and explicit white text treatment; the source relies on both to keep the centered message legible over changing footage.
