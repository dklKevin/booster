## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Give an airline a recognizable visual flightpath without literal aircraft motifs: repeat one controlled violet family across navigation states, atmospheric section gradients, and interaction feedback.
- Pair an expressive display face only with major headings, then let a purpose-built bilingual sans carry navigation, body copy, and Arabic; this preserves character without weakening operational clarity.
- Alternate full-bleed media with tightly governed 5%/15% insets and a 1440px cap, so cinematic storytelling stays legible on both ordinary and very wide screens.
- Make destination and brand storytelling explorable through a consistent rounded-tile interaction: imagery remains primary until a deliberate plus action reveals detail in place.
- Keep editorial pages structurally quieter than the homepage: the article source switches to a #f6f7fc reading surface and 13% desktop side padding while retaining the same type and color system.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy every purple gradient at once; the source assigns different ramps to CTAs, section backgrounds, and tile reveals, and collapsing those roles would turn hierarchy into visual noise.
- Do not reuse the long 0.55-0.99s staged tile transitions for booking or other task-critical controls; they suit exploratory brand content, not time-sensitive utility flows.
