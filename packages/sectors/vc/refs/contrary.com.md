## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Make portfolio credibility architectural: the same column rules organize founder portraits, company logos, research cards, team profiles, and the portfolio directory instead of relying on decorative badges.
- Reserve the saturated `#184DF6` for decisive moments - CTA text, buttons, small section markers, and one full-width thesis band - so it remains high-signal against warm neutrals.
- Pair large plainspoken FK Grotesk headlines with 12–14px FK Grotesk Mono labels and actions; the contrast separates institutional narrative from navigational metadata without adding another color.
- Keep interior directories scannable by retaining the outer 1344px frame and ruled grid while changing column density (6 to 11/12) to match the content.
- Use generous section padding (`80–128px`) around thesis statements, then compress repeated records into rule-separated rows; this creates alternating persuasion and evidence rhythms.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Copying the many 1px rules without strict column alignment would produce visual noise; their effect depends on edges continuing across navigation, sections, cards, and directory rows.
- Using `#184DF6` broadly would erase the hierarchy created by its sparse use and make the warm `#F1F0ED`/`#EDECE9` foundation feel generic.
