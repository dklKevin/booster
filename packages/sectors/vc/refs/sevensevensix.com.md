## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Make the firm thesis the largest object: the 100px hero states the operating model directly, while proof follows as a dense, bordered 60-column stats grid rather than generic portfolio imagery.
- Let one motion system carry the identity across sections: the same 40-frame form responds to scroll, converges toward the mark, and hands off to the word-by-word manifesto.
- Pair very wide display composition with deliberately narrower evidence: 1740px canvases create presence, while 830–1100px copy measures and a 900px article column preserve reading comfort.
- Use restrained near-neutrals for the system and reserve `#776BCE` for meaningful accents; hierarchy comes primarily from scale, weight, rules, and motion instead of many colors.
- Treat portfolio density as an index: responsive 320px cells, hairline separators, centered emoji/name/URL, and a subtle 5% black hover make many companies scannable without visual competition.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the 100px hero or 290px manifesto padding without the same responsive reductions; the source drops the hero to 69px and then 40px, and switches page gutters from 70px to 25px.
- Do not imitate the 40-frame scroll animation as decoration alone: without its resolution into the 776 mark and timed manifesto reveal, the asset weight and motion would obscure rather than reinforce the thesis.
