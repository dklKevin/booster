## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Make credibility feel consequential through scale: one plain, enormous mission statement carries the hero while body copy stays at restrained 16–20px sizes.
- Separate categories with desaturated, named institutional colors rather than tactical camouflage; Evergreen, Atlas, Archive, Cloud, and Foundary remain serious while making the system navigable.
- Pair expansive cinematic media with rigid content limits and a 4px spacing grid; the contrast keeps a dramatic defense story orderly and legible.
- Use uppercase mono only for compact labels and taxonomy, leaving Aeonik’s neutral sans forms to carry claims, evidence, and calls to action.
- Preserve the same token system across structures: the homepage uses motion and three-card storytelling, while the public-sector page swaps in rounded full-bleed imagery without becoming a separate visual brand.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Copying only the huge headline without the short line length, ample media field, and restrained supporting copy would produce a loud but shallow defense aesthetic.
- Applying every accent together would weaken the hierarchy; the fetched pages use colored surfaces and label accents selectively against dominant black, white, and gray.
