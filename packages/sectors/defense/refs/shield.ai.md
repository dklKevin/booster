## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Let full-viewport, autoplay-muted-looping product footage carry the first impression, then anchor it with one centered product name, one concise capability line, and one action; the homepage repeats this exact frame for X-BAT, Hivemind, and V-BAT.
- Pair an expressive large grotesk display face with a compact monospaced uppercase utility face; the shift in typography separates mission narrative from interface metadata without adding more colors.
- Make the system feel technical through a small, repeatable construction detail: Shield AI reuses its corner brackets on buttons, navigation, banners, and other controls rather than decorating every section differently.
- Alternate immersive #050506 media sections with #EAEAEA information surfaces while holding typography and container geometry constant; product and company pages remain related despite different content structures.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the oversized type without the extracted responsive clamps: a fixed 76–164px display size would overwhelm the 16px mobile gutters.
- Do not combine the full-screen video slider, very large headings, orange hover state, and bracket chrome with additional military/HUD decoration; the source keeps the palette and control geometry deliberately sparse.
