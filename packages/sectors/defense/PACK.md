# defense sector package

Use this pack for defense, aerospace, autonomy, and national-security sites where operational credibility must coexist with clear product storytelling.
Load with the form package matching the page's job, plus 2-3 refs total chosen for the brief.
Refs teach how to think, never what to build.

## The defense mode (avoid)

- Avoid the full-bleed autoplay hero under a dark gradient, carrying oversized white mission copy, a generic “Learn More” action, and a scroll cue.
- Avoid reflexive institutional navy and action blue, especially Boeing `#0033a1`, General Dynamics `#003087`, Lockheed Martin `#0360ae`, or the familiar black-and-white system with RTX red `#CE1126`.
- Avoid making Air / Land / Sea / Space / Cyber the homepage’s dominant equal-width photographic taxonomy.
- Avoid dense sequences of 16:9 hardware cards, three-up business grids, image-and-copy bands, news carousels, and Products / Programs / Services triptychs.
- Avoid treating aircraft, weapons, satellites, ships, technicians, factories, and patriotic footage as interchangeable edge-to-edge atmosphere beneath dark overlays.
- Avoid condensed or widely tracked uppercase technical labels as the default voice, from Univers and DIN to Futura-style display copy.
- Avoid staging reassurance mainly through named programs, contract values, order counts, century-scale history, global reach, stock quotes, and isolated safety or governance bands.
- Avoid the heavy black, navy, or gradient mega-footer where investor, supplier, governance, policy, social, legal, and stock information becomes the final proof of scale.

## Tells

Full-bleed military footage plus a dark fade, huge white mission language, and a “Learn More” CTA instantly reads as sector default.
Five equal Air / Land / Sea / Space / Cyber image tiles signal a capability catalog before they communicate a point of view.
Named weapons and contract figures accumulate above an investor-and-governance mega-footer as a substitute for structured evidence.
Institutional navy, signal orange or blue rectangles, and condensed uppercase labels turn every interaction into familiar prime-contractor chrome.

## Register

- Load-bearing trust comes from concrete operational evidence such as named systems, dated program claims, production capacity, order or contract figures, and live usage counts, not accreditation decoration absent from the surveyed homepages.
- Corporate history, public-company data, governance, supplier, E-Verify, and EEO links matter as continuity and accountability signals, but they should support the argument rather than constitute it.
- The audience expects concise, technically literate, mission-aware language; compact mono or uppercase labels can carry taxonomy while sentence-case display and readable sans-serif copy carry claims.
- Excellence proves that authority can come from rigorous grids, narrow reading measures, disciplined type scale, and sparse state color rather than navy bands, card density, or faux-tactical ornament.
- Cinematic hardware imagery remains credible when paired with quiet evidence surfaces, exact content limits, and a small number of repeatable motion or control gestures.
- High-energy colors such as Anduril `#dff140`, Helsing `#F36702`, Saronic `#acff24`, and Shield AI `#FF6343` work as scarce signals for state, telemetry, focus, or hover rather than broad decoration.
- **Operational surface:** keep named systems, dated programme claims, production or contract facts, careers, and governance or supplier routes on the owned site. Surveyed excellence pages treat those as evidence surfaces, not footer residue.
- **Operational consistency:** programme names, dates, and capacity figures must agree across homepage, product, and investor pages. Century-scale history and stock quotes cannot replace a current system page.
- **Photography:** caption specific hardware, factories, or telemetry. Interchangeable aircraft, ships, and patriotic footage under a dark gradient is the mode.
