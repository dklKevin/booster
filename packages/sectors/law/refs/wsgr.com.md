## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Pair a precise sans display face with a readable serif body face so institutional authority does not require defaulting to an all-serif law-firm aesthetic.
- Reserve the compact navy/white/teal/coral palette for strong field changes and small accents; the hierarchy remains legible even across image-heavy modules.
- Use one geometric motif at several controlled depths (30px, 35px, 45px, 75px) to connect captions, photography, and section transitions without adding ornamental clutter.
- Let editorial modules break into 6-, 8-, and 10-column compositions inside a stable 1440px shell; this creates variety while preserving alignment.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Copying every angled-edge variant at once would turn a distinctive motif into visual noise; the source applies named directions and sizes to specific media/caption roles.
- The 96px display tier and 80px desktop gutters need the extracted responsive reductions; using them unchanged on small screens would overwhelm content.
