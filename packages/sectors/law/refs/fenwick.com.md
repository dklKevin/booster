## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Make proof of expertise the visual centerpiece: Fenwick turns transactions and case results into large editorial image tiles instead of relegating them to a conventional news list.
- Pair institutional restraint with one assertive brand color: #0475de carries heroes, links, rules and actions while #313134, #1c1d21 and white keep long legal content composed.
- Use an expanded grotesk, tight negative tracking and a steep 40-to-80px display ramp to project confidence without relying on traditional law-firm serif conventions.
- Preserve readable interior-page structure while varying the hero: practice pages use a blue split/tile hero, while publications use a dark #1c1d1e header and a constrained left content column.
- Build motion cues into static styling: staggered tile depths, 1.05 image scale, 40px hover translations, outlined marquee copy and dotted underlines make dense credentials feel active.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the extreme negative offsets and 35vh/15rem tile spacing without the same responsive reordering rules; the composition can overlap, strand whitespace or scramble reading order.
- Do not apply the 5rem display size or expanded face to article body headings; Fenwick explicitly drops rich-text headings to roughly 1.375-2.5rem for long-form readability.
