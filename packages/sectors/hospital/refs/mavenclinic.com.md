## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Treat the care continuum as a visual system: keep one stable hero structure while rotating precise life-stage messages and imagery, so breadth reads as coordinated rather than fragmented.
- Pair clinical credibility with warmth by anchoring navigation, text, and CTAs in deep greens while using warm natural section grounds instead of default hospital white.
- Use large, light-weight sans display type for confident claims, then reserve editorial and mono faces for human storytelling and compact labels; the hierarchy stays expressive without weakening scanability.
- Build spacing and section density from explicit presets: compact 48–80px modules can carry proof, while 120–240px transitions give major narrative shifts room to register.
- Keep conversion paths role-specific in the first viewport: the source separates “Explore platform” from “Get care,” serving buyers and members without collapsing their intent.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the four-state animated hero without tight image crops, restrained transition timing, and a static/mobile fallback; the combined Rive, pill, and photo motion can otherwise obscure the healthcare promise.
- Do not apply every bright life-stage accent everywhere; without the deep-green and natural neutral anchors, the palette would read as consumer wellness rather than credible clinical care.
