## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Give science brands distinct editorial voices inside one family: condensed headings carry urgency, serif copy adds humanity, and sans-serif UI preserves clinical clarity.
- Reserve the institutional blue for actions and orientation, then use warm-gray content fields to keep long scientific and corporate pages from feeling sterile.
- Pair immersive storytelling with disciplined interiors: the homepage can use viewport-scale animation while reference-heavy pages return to a capped grid, rails, and 18px/30px reading text.
- Encode whitespace as section-level utilities (30/40/60/80/130px), so dense medical information and campaign storytelling share a predictable vertical cadence.
- Make the brand itself the interaction: the masked wordmark connects motion and imagery to Genentech rather than relying on a generic pharma hero treatment.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the 100lvh sticky wordmark reveal without lightweight responsive imagery and a simpler fallback; its impact depends on bespoke logo geometry and scroll choreography.
- Do not apply the condensed display face or 50–100px campaign scale to long medical copy; the source confines those choices to headings and immersive CTA scenes.
