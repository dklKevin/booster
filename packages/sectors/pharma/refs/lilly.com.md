## Lessons (3-5 bullets)
- Let human, full-bleed photography carry emotional authority while a controlled gradient preserves legibility; this keeps a medicine company warm without weakening information hierarchy.
- Use one expressive serif as an intentional alternate voice inside a rigorous sans system: the homepage explicitly switches hero titles between Ringside and Garamond while retaining shared sizing and grid behavior.
- Encode density as section modes: 144px desktop/96px tablet “wide” padding and 64px desktop/40px tablet “narrow” padding create repeatable pacing across the homepage and structurally different condition and research pages.
- Keep long-form health content on 8–10 of 12 columns while allowing hero media to go full bleed; the contrast makes clinical detail readable without making the brand experience feel document-like.
- Pair a restrained core of red, black, white, and quiet tints with semantic colors reserved for feedback states, so the company color remains unmistakable without coloring every module red.

## Avoid (1-2 bullets)
- Do not copy the 900px hero or 100px title without the responsive reductions (720/612px hero variants and 40–60px heading defaults); otherwise key content and calls to action will be pushed below the fold.
- Do not use the full extended palette decoratively just because it exists in the token file; the source assigns many hues to semantic roles while the visible brand foundation stays tightly red/neutral.
