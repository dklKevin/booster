# pharma sector package

Use this pack for corporate pharma, biotech, R&D, pipeline, patient-impact, and investor-facing web experiences where scientific authority must coexist with institutional trust.
Load with the form package matching the page's job, plus 2-3 refs total chosen for the brief.
Refs teach how to think, never what to build.

## The pharma mode (avoid)

- Avoid opening on a giant split carousel or 70-95vh autoplay video that makes quarterly results the primary story.
- Avoid treating white or off-white panels, near-black copy, and one saturated corporate action color as a complete identity, whether that color is GSK orange `#f36633`, Merck teal `#00857c`, Pfizer violet-blue `#2e29ff`, Roche blue `#0b41cd`, or Novartis orange `#ff4e00`.
- Avoid the modular march of equal-weight cards, alternating 50/50 stripes, article carousels, therapeutic-area gateways, investor modules, and newsletter bands.
- Avoid the stock image library of pipettes, microscopes, gloved lab work, molecular renders, polished scientists, campuses, and warm patient-family portraits.
- Avoid making oversized figures such as GSK's “7m,” Pfizer's 95-program total, or Roche's “CHF 30.4 billion” interchangeable card content.
- Avoid using results, share prices, annual reports, CEO quotations, impact reports, and longevity claims as decorative reassurance.
- Avoid a dominant footer packed with product and trial links, regional disclaimers, patient resources, SEC caveats, social channels, and country routing.

## Tells

- A giant mission headline overlays cells, laboratory machinery, or a darkened corporate video and resolves into a “Learn more” button.
- Patient warmth, lab science, ESG, careers, and investor results are normalized into the same carousel-and-card grammar.
- Pipeline or disease-burden figures become visual proof beside a molecular animation or biomedical backdrop.
- A purpose promise and caregiver photograph give way to an oversized legal, reporting, and audience-routing footer.

## Register

- Load-bearing trust is current, dated evidence: pipeline and trial access, product information, results, reports, patient resources, and precise claims.
- Legal, safety, regional-use, accessibility, and forward-looking disclosures are infrastructure, while social icons, longevity slogans, and CEO reassurance are not proof.
- The audience expects calm, exact, globally legible language that distinguishes ambition from evidence and connects patient benefit to a mechanism, program, or result.
- Credible distinctiveness is structural: Genentech's wordmark mask, insitro's repeated hexagons, and Isomorphic Labs' gridded molecular interfaces are systems, not decoration.
- Lilly's editorial serif, Moderna's ordered collage, and Recursion's lab footage prove that emotional or cinematic range can retain information discipline.
- Brand color should orient meaning through stable action, content, and semantic roles rather than flood modules.
