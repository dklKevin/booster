# Gwern (gwern.net, extracted 2026-08-06)

- Grayscale by policy: light bg #fff/text #000/surfaces #f8f8f8 to #d8d8d8 by nesting depth; dark bg #161616 (never pure black: scroll jank, harsh contrast), text #f1f1f1.
- With no hue available, every distinction is typographic: small-caps page title, right-aligned h1 with solid rule, uppercase h2 with dotted rule, dropcaps, weight, spacing. Grayscale is a constraint that forces work, not a style.
- Type: Source Serif 4 body at 20px, sans for UI/metadata, mono for code; line-height stepped 1.45/1.50/1.55/1.60 across four viewport brackets.
- Layout: 935px column; sidenotes in both margins (activate above ~1760px, degrade to floating footnotes below); justified + hyphenated above 650px only.
- Semantic zoom: one page readable at every depth: title, abstract, headers, margin notes, body, collapsed sections, popups. Design the ladder before the page.
- Link affordances encode meaning: underline style predicts what a click does (plain / preview / annotated popup); icons preview destination type.
- Signature: the margin as a first-class column; references at eye level, never a jump to the bottom.

Avoid: the ornament density (dropcaps, small caps, link icons) ships alongside a reader mode and retiring demo mode; imitate the ornament without the off-switches and it reads as noise.
