# Document package

For research docs, study guides, reviews, long-form reading. Most of our artifacts live here.
Load with the installed DESIGN.md plus 2-3 files from `refs/` chosen for the brief. Never load the whole refs folder.
Refs teach how to think, never what to build.
Deeper background: `../../notes/gwern.md`.

## Range map

- `refs/gwern.md`: dense-austere; grayscale scholarship, the margin as a first-class column.
- `refs/maggie-appleton.md`: warm-illustrated; hand-drawn diagrams doing scholarly work.

## Lessons (cross-site)

- Density is a navigation problem, not a layout problem: build a ladder of depth (abstract, headers, margin notes, body, collapse, popup) so one page serves the skimmer and the completist.
- Encode meaning in the affordance itself: visual differences should be semantic differences (underline styles that predict the click, framing that marks screenshots vs drawings).
- Warmth is one reusable motion primitive repeated everywhere, not many bespoke effects.
- Push work to build time; the reading experience must survive with JS off.
- Design pays off multiplicatively across every page that exists and ever will; writing pays off additively. Polish is rational at scale.

## Avoid (cross-site)

- Density without escape hatches (reader mode, toggles) is noise; grayscale without the typographic labor is flat text.
- Accents spread beyond their single jobs turn scholarly into craft-fair.
- Margin-note machinery without its narrow-screen fallback breaks most real readers.
