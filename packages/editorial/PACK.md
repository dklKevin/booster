# Editorial package

For showcase and presentation pages built on ideas: landing pages, pitch docs, book-like pages, anything meant to impress with content rather than a physical object.
Load with the installed DESIGN.md plus files from `refs/` chosen for the brief. Never load the whole refs folder.
Refs teach how to think, never what to build.

## Range map

- `refs/practicaltypography.md`: bookish typographic quiet; calibrated type can make reading itself the demonstration.
- `refs/browsercompany.md`: sparse ambient presence; a single responsive layer can enliven an otherwise restrained composition.
- `refs/stripe-press.md`: object-led color stage; a committed content-specific pair can unify an entire presentation.
- `refs/asterisk.md`: issue-led literary magazine; varied type remains coherent when every contrast has an editorial role.
- `refs/worksinprogress.md`: rule-dense periodical; taxonomy, grids, and disciplined metadata make high content density legible.
- `refs/creativeindependent.md`: modular chromatic archive; controlled per-story color systems can support variety without losing identity.
- `refs/oxide.md`: technical dark system; subject-derived visual language can connect explanation, interface, and atmosphere.
- `refs/mercury.md`: cinematic product narrative; coordinated motion and chapter shifts can turn complex flows into a paced story.

## Lessons (cross-site)

- Execution quality is restraint plus depth in exactly one place: a tiny vocabulary with all discretionary effort spent on one centerpiece. Nothing is decorated twice.
- Color commitment works best when it carries content meaning: assign a controlled pair to a book, story, issue, or chapter, then propagate it consistently through states and structure.
- Editorial hierarchy can come from assigned roles across serif, sans, mono, optical cuts, italics, and weight rather than from size alone.
- Dense pages stay legible when reading measure, metadata, rules, and navigation each occupy a clearly bounded layer.
- A centerpiece earns its weight only when it represents the actual thing being offered; its fallback must be authored, not inherited.
- Motion should reveal presence, state, or process, stay subordinate to reading, and yield cleanly when reduced motion is requested.

## Avoid (cross-site)

- Untethered accent variety produces decorative noise; color needs a content taxonomy or a committed local pair.
- A heavy interactive centerpiece without a real relationship to the subject is a performance bill disguised as engagement.
- Borrowing surface shorthand such as cream serif minimalism, dark mono tech, or bright editorial tiles without the underlying content system produces genre cosplay.
